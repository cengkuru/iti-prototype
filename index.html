Is infrastructure transparency actually working?

The ITI measures how well governments publish, explain, and use infrastructure information across the full project lifecycle, turning transparency promises into comparable evidence for reform.

Why another index exists

Most systems stop at “was it published?” The ITI asks the harder question.

Incomplete data
Records exist but miss the fields that matter for oversight.
Weak capacity
Institutions lack the systems to sustain publication over time.
Low citizen use
Data is online but never reaches the people it should serve.
No benchmark
Without a comparable score, no country can see how it stands.

Our global vision

From single assessments to a global benchmark.

The ITI is being built as infrastructure, not a one-off report: a phased path, region by region.

Foundation

Single-country implementations

Proven assessments establishing the method and the first comparable scores.

Regional pilots

Latin America, East Africa, Southeast Asia

Clusters of countries assessed together, building local capacity to run the ITI.

Expanded coverage

More regions, more local ownership

Wider regional coverage with assessment capacity held inside each region.

Global benchmark

100+ countries by 2031

A globally comparable measure of whether infrastructure transparency works.

An ambition, not a guarantee. The 100-country target is the scaling pathway in the ITI Business Plan. It is the direction of travel, and it depends on the financing and partnerships to get there.
